Questions?
Answered.

Everything you need to know about Aether, billing, and how we handle your data.

Aether combines sub-second query performance, a genuinely beautiful interface, and native AI features in one product. Most tools ask you to choose between speed, design, and intelligence. Aether delivers all three without compromise. We also include SSO, audit logs, and enterprise-grade security on our Team plan — features typically locked behind enterprise contracts elsewhere.
Aether supports 42+ native integrations including PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, MongoDB, Stripe, Segment, Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Analytics, Shopify, and more. If your source isn't natively supported, our REST API lets you push data from anywhere.
Every new account gets a full 14-day trial of the Team plan — no credit card required. You get unlimited dashboards, all integrations, and full feature access. When the trial ends, you can downgrade to Starter (free forever) or upgrade to Team. No data is deleted.
Yes. Aether is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We support SSO, SCIM provisioning, audit logs, and role-based access. We never train models on customer data, and we never sell or share data with third parties. Enterprise customers can request a custom BAA for HIPAA workloads.
Absolutely. Aether subscriptions are monthly or annual with no cancellation fees. You can cancel from your dashboard at any time. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period, then move to the free Starter plan.
Yes. Early-stage startups (under $1M raised, fewer than 20 employees) qualify for 50% off the Team plan for 12 months. Registered nonprofits and educational institutions get the Team plan free. Reach out via the contact page and we'll set you up.
We'll never cut off your access mid-month. Instead, we'll notify you when you reach 80% of your limit and add overage billing at $0.001 per query. If you'd rather not have overages, you can set a hard limit in your billing settings.
Self-hosting is available for Enterprise customers as a licensed deployment. If you need VPC deployment or on-premises installation for compliance reasons, contact sales to discuss options and pricing.
